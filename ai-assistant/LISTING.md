- **Long description:**
  Walkito shows short foot and calf exercises with videos, in English.
  - *Relief now:* a gentle 3-minute seated routine for a foot that hurts today.
  - *First-step stretch:* the plantar fascia stretch done in bed before standing up.
  - *Starter plan:* a 7-day plan from the painful area, minutes a day and equipment.
  - *Exercise demo:* a short video and cues for one exercise.
  - *Flat foot check:* the big toe lift check and what the result usually means.
  - *When to see a doctor:* the warning signs that mean getting checked.
  - *Feet after work:* a short routine after a shift, and shoe tips.
  - *Shoes and inserts:* general footwear tips, no brands.
  - *Ready to run:* a short readiness check before running again.

  Walkito is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. We don't store anything you type here.
