## 1. Bottom line

- **Walkito's onboarding is not the leak. The hand-off from "plan ready" to price is.** 97% of Meta users finish onboarding on 1.0.11+ (R1), which is better than most apps. Then ~57% leave on paywall page 1. Top apps put their hardest work into the 2-3 screens right before the price: a concrete personal result, then the offer. Walkito's 3 screens before the price all say "your plan is ready" but none shows a concrete result. [verified screens, guess on cause]
- **Walkito's 3-page paywall is a near copy of Cal AI's** ("We want you to try Cal AI for free / No Payment Due Now" -> "We'll send a reminder before your trial ends" -> plans). It works for Cal AI: 57% of its paywall viewers start a purchase (Superwall, reported). But Cal AI's users are young TikTok users who just saw a plan with exact numbers and a goal date, and created an account. Walkito's are women 55+ from Facebook who just saw "Right shin, First steps 5/10". Same paywall, very different context. [verified screens + reported numbers]
- **Multi-page vs single-page paywalls are a real split.** Across 40M onboarding paywall opens, multi-page converted 12.41% vs 9.07% single-page (Superwall, May 2026). But **Stompers, a walking app, tested it and single-page won by 16%** with more yearly trials. Walkito's own data (57% gone on page 1, before any price) points the same way as Stompers. [reported]
- **Every top health app shows a concrete personal result right before the paywall:** Cal AI "Goal: lose 10 lbs by October 11" + 1,266 calories, Rise "your sleep need" number, Opal "your screen time report", Noom/Simple a weight-by-date graph (3 times), Finch "Ali's starter plan" with 7 easy goals, Speak "In 2 months you'll communicate while traveling in France". Walkito can't use date/outcome promises (health ad rules), but it already owns the best legal version: **the Foot Check numbers** (ankle bend, balance seconds). Today 2 of 5 results are locked. Top apps give the result and sell the plan. [verified]
- **Length is not the problem.** H&F median is 20.5 screens (Lazyweb, n=26). Cal AI 29-45, Noom ~45 steps, Rise 60, Lose It 70. Time to paywall in recordings: Calm 0:42, Hallow 0:54, Headspace 1:01, Cal AI ~3:20, Flo 4:22, Fastic 6:51, Noom 7:38. Walkito's ~6 min sits with the weight-loss apps. Lose It and a teen learning app both report longer onboarding raised day-one subs. [verified timelines, reported tests]
- **The first price appears late everywhere, but never after a "gift" screen with no product on it.** Cal AI's no-price page 1 shows the product (phone scanning food). Duolingo's says "$0 for 1 month". Finch's says "Finch is free to use. But we'd love you to try Plus". Walkito's page 1 shows a cartoon holding a gift box. [verified]
- **Two cheap fixes the leaders all do and Walkito doesn't:** ask "How did you hear about us?" in the first 3-6 screens (Cal AI step 3, Duolingo 4, Rise 4, Flo 6; Walkito ~19), and make the opening screen show the product working (Cal AI scan animation). ~35% of Walkito installs leave in the first 3 screens (R1). [verified]
- **Top recommendation:** replace the 3 "plan ready" screens + 3-page paywall with: Foot Check result (all 5 visible) -> plan with the user's own goal and words -> one single-page paywall (timeline + yearly/monthly + "Not now"), with Day 1 offered after "Not now". Expected effect: more people reach a price (target >70% of paywall viewers, from ~38%). [guess]

Patterns I can count from this table [verified]:
- **Paywall placement:** all 18 show it at the end of (or inside) the first-run flow. None makes you use the core product first. Only Duolingo used to (lesson first), and the current captures show the offer before the lesson.
- **Length splits by category, not by revenue.** Meditation/faith (Calm, Hallow, Headspace) reach the price in about 1 minute. Weight loss and women's health (Noom, Fastic, Flo, Simple) take 3.5-7.5 minutes. Both make millions.
- **"Building your plan" loader:** 11 of 18 have one. **Personal result screen right before the price:** 11 of 18. Lazyweb says only 9 of ~751 apps overall do a loader, so this is a health/self-improvement pattern, not a general one [reported, Lazyweb July 2026].
- **"How did you hear about us":** in 7 of 18, usually in the first 6 screens (Cal AI step 3, Duolingo 4, Rise 4, Flo 6, Bible Chat ~11, Noom near the end, Finch after purchase).
- **ATT placement:** first screen (Speak, Ladder, Bible Chat, Calm 2nd), during the loader (Cal AI), mid-quiz (Flo, Duolingo, Rise), right before the plan (Fastic), after purchase (Hallow). No single rule.
- **Commitment screen right before the paywall:** Flo, Opal (and Duolingo's "commitment level" question).
- **Account before paywall:** 9 of 18. Lazyweb: when a flow has both, 16 of 17 put the account first [reported]. Cal AI frames it as "Save your progress" for the plan you just saw.

### 2.4 The patterns, with numbers

**Screens before paywall**
- Median onboarding is 11 screens overall, **20.5 in Health & Fitness** (n=26), 15 in Education. Only 9% of flows reach 30+ [reported, Lazyweb Jul 2026].
- Mobbin (1,460 flows): average app has 25 onboarding screens; 22% show a paywall during onboarding [reported, Apr 2026].
- 54% of H&F onboarding flows contain a paywall, at 84% of the way through the flow [reported, Lazyweb].

**Length tests**
- Lose It kept adding questions "until we got diminishing returns"; trial start rate up by double digits [reported, RevenueCat via LiftArchive].
- A teen AI learning app went from 10 to 30+ screens: 2.5x day-one subscriptions, +23% D1-D5 retention, "100+ A/B tests" [reported, @_jouv1 on X, Nov 11, 2026].
- Counter-examples: an education app removed its onboarding loading screen: trial conversion +22%, ARPU +30% [reported, Adapty, Nov 2025]. Monzo cut sign-up from 39 to 17 screens and doubled completions (fintech) [reported, Bogdan Stefanovic video, Nov 2026].
- LiftArchive's rule: there is no correct count; measure install-to-trial, not paywall conversion, because a long flow can lift paywall conversion by self-selection while losing people before it.

**Question types**
- Cal AI: maths inputs first ("used to calibrate your plan"), motivation questions after.
- Flo's lesson: one new question (perimenopause phase) lost ~10% twice. 75% of users could not be assigned a phase because the wording was hard. Rewritten with their medical team and skipping silently when unknown: **+23%** on the third try [reported, Flo's Mike Charniatsou, Sub Club, Nov 11, 2026].
- Flo also built a separate onboarding branch matching one ad's story ("we acknowledge the user came from a specific ad narrative") [reported, same].
- Headspace let users pick more than one goal: +10% free-trial conversion [reported, Mobbin video, Apr 2026].
- Thomas Cotter (Sub Club Live, Nov 12, 2026): a scaled H&F app got **+36% ARPU from persona-matched paywalls** (hero line, design and features change with one quiz answer). Advice: pick a persona that is at least 25% of installs; "change the answers, not just the question" [reported].
- Adapty: a productivity app with personalized onboarding vs generic: trial starts +8.5%, paying +17%, ARPU +22% (US paying +27%) [reported, Nov 2025].

**Progress bars**: all of Cal AI, Duolingo, Hallow, Opal, Noom show a thin top bar (screens saved). QUITTR numbers its quiz "#2 of 10" with a "Skip test" link [reported, ASOitis]. Duolingo says up front "Just 7 quick questions before we start your first lesson!" [reported, Botsi].

**Social proof placement**: Cal AI puts it right before the notification ask; Rise and Opal near the start; Noom mid-quiz plus a testimonial; Fastic just before ATT; Bible Chat on screen 4. Social proof is on 53% of captured apps in 2025, up from 26% in 2023 [reported, Lazyweb], but only on ~6% of paywalls themselves [reported, Lazyweb].

**Loading / "building your plan" screen**: 11 of my 18 apps. Cal AI's runs 0-95% with lines like "Applying BMR formula". Speak's shows the mascot "Generating your curriculum" [reported, Lazyweb]. One counter-test above (removing a loader +22%).

**Plan reveal**: the strong ones show something specific to *you*: a number (Rise sleep need, Cal AI calories), a date (Noom, Simple, Cal AI, QUITTR "You will quit by Jan 4, 2027"), or a short list built from your answers (Finch, Speak, Duolingo). Weak ones (Calm, Headspace, Hallow) skip it and rely on brand.

**Commitment screens**: Flo and Opal right before the paywall; Duolingo asks "commitment level"; Finch has one after purchase.

**Notification + ATT**: notification primers are on 40% of apps [reported, Lazyweb, n=809]. Calm asks for reminders after the first meditation: 40% opt in vs ~1% who found them in settings before [reported, Bogdan Stefanovic video]. An education app that set up reminders inside onboarding got +6.4% trial conversion and +24% ARPU [reported, Adapty].

**First "aha"**: in all 18 recordings the paywall comes before real product use. The "aha" before the price is a *simulated* one: plan, number, graph, demo. Exceptions are demos inside onboarding (Fastic timer demo, Blinkist swipe game). Adam Lyttle is testing a "use the app inside onboarding, then pay" design but has no result yet [reported, Mar 2026].

**Where the first price appears**: Cal AI page 3 of the paywall; Bible Chat page 2 (after "Free Trial Information"); Duolingo page 1 says "$0 for 1 month"; Calm, Hallow, Headspace, Opal, Rise, Noom page 1.

**How they handle "no"**
- Cal AI: no "Not now" on pages 1-2; transaction-abandon paywalls, win-back flows, spin-wheel discount; 46 trigger points in total [reported, Superwall].
- Flo: a "special offer" paywall right after the first one [verified chapter list].
- Fastic: discount code screen right before the paywall [verified chapter list].
- Finch / Duolingo: free tier behind the X [verified screens].
- App Masters: a no-trial soft paywall early, then 3 trial-explainer screens ("try for free", "we'll remind you", "how your trial works"), then a second paywall with a 3-day trial and social proof. Start rate 3% -> 4.4% -> 7.7%, sales ~$1K -> ~$3K/mo; "not a clean test" [reported, GTM Sauce / App Masters, Sep 2026].
- BoldVoice: hard paywall with no close button got more trials, but refunds grew more than the gain [reported, LiftArchive].

**Multi-page paywall**
- Superwall, 40M+ onboarding paywall opens Feb-May 2026: multi-page 12.41% vs single-page 9.07% (+37%); multi-page is only 24% of opens [reported].
- Speak4Me: Duolingo-style multi-step paywall +27% conversion, +36% proceeds [reported, Superwall, Jan 2026].
- **Stompers (walking + step-counter app): single-page beat multi-page (benefits + trial reminder before price) by 16%, and drove more yearly trials.** Prices tested: $9.99/mo vs $79.99 or $49.99/yr [reported, Superwall, Jan 2026].
- Rosie Hoggmascall: paywall after onboarding 2% opt-in; paywall before onboarding 8%; 3-slide carousel then paywall 15% [reported, RevenueCat via LiftArchive].

## 4. What Walkito should copy / test (ranked)

Effort: S = copy/config, M = 1-3 days, L = a week+. Effects are **[guess]** unless marked. Volume is ~10-15 paywall views/day, so treat these as sequential version changes measured on "% of paywall viewers who reach prices" and "buy taps", not as clean A/B tests.

1. **Merge paywall pages 1-3 into one page (Stompers pattern).** Keep: headline about *their* plan, the Today/Day 5/Day 7 timeline, yearly (show "$5.00/mo") + monthly, "Start my free week", "Not now", full terms. Drop the gift-box and alarm-clock pages. Why: 57% leave page 1 before any price; Stompers (walking app) +16% single-page [reported]; Walkito's page 2 and 3 already repeat page 1. Expected: share reaching the price from ~38% to 70%+. Effort S-M. **Rank 1.**
2. **Turn screen 1 into a real result: show the Foot Check numbers.** "Your ankle bend: 31° (right) / 38° (left). Your balance: 12 seconds." Then "Your plan starts with these 2 seated moves for your ankle." Unlock all 5 results; lock the *plan*, not the result. Make the Foot Check default for everyone (not flag-gated). Why: Rise / Opal / Cal AI pattern; your own value with no claim of cure or time. Expected: biggest lift on page-1 exits. Effort M. **Rank 2.** Wording guard: no "fix/heal/relieve/pain-free", no dates.
3. **Rewrite the plan reveal in the user's words, and fix the off details.** Use their answers: "For: heel pain in the morning (you said 6/10) · on your feet all day · goal: walk the dog without stopping". Replace "Right shin / First steps 5/10" with plain words, remove "Choosing your first week..." and the loader-looking bars, show full text. One "plan is ready" headline, not three. Effort S. **Rank 3.**
4. **Use the real-foot image on the paywall, not the gift mascot.** Cal AI's no-price page shows the product working; Walkito's best image (`IMG_6944`) is on the screen before. For a 55+ audience a real foot doing a seated move says "this is real exercise" [guess]. Effort S. **Rank 4.**
5. **Day 1 order: one choice, not two "Not now"s.** Option A (my pick): plan reveal -> single paywall -> "Not now" -> "Try the first 2 minutes now" -> Day 1 -> prices. Option B: Day 1 first for everyone, then paywall. Either way, never two "Not now" taps in a row. Effort S. **Rank 5.**
6. **Move "How did you hear about us?" to step 2-3.** Every leader does it early (Cal AI 3, Duolingo 4, Rise 4, Flo 6). It also lets you measure the 35% who leave in the first 3 screens by source. Adapty saw ~+10% for one fitness client [reported, single anecdote]. Effort S. **Rank 6.**
7. **First screen = product working + ad continuity.** A 5-second loop of real feet doing an ankle rock, one line ("Short daily foot exercises you can do sitting down"), one button. If the ad is the cartoon mascot, show the same mascot saying hi on screen 1 (Flo's ad-narrative branch idea). Target: cut the 35% first-3-screen loss. Effort S-M. **Rank 7.**
8. **Persona-matched paywall headline (one quiz answer).** "On my feet all day" -> "Your 5-minute after-shift routine"; "heel pain in the morning" -> "Your 5-minute morning heel routine"; "nothing hurts / walking more" -> "Your 5-minute walking-feet routine". +36% ARPU reported in a scaled H&F app [reported]. Only worth it for personas that are 25%+ of installs. Effort S-M. **Rank 8.**
9. **Honest social proof once, before the notification ask (Cal AI spot).** With 4 ratings you cannot do "Join 10 million". Use what is true: PT review (already there), "60+ filmed moves" (or the real count), "Made for people who stand all day". No made-up numbers, no fake testimonials. Effort S. **Rank 9.**
10. **Show a per-month price on yearly.** Cal AI shows "$2.49/mo" next to "$29.99 billed annually". "$59.99/yr (about $5 a month)". Effort S. **Rank 10.**

Do NOT copy: Cal AI's no-"Not now" pages, spin wheels, countdowns, weight-by-date graphs (timeframe claims), "without a plan" curves that imply results, rating prompts before use (QUITTR). Do not cut onboarding questions on the strength of "shorter is better"; Walkito's completion is already 97%.
