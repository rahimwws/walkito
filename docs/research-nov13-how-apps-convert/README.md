# How successful apps convert, and why Walkito does not (research, Nov 13, 2026)

8 AI research agents + 1 checker (Claude Opus 5.5), read-only. Question 1: is Walkito's 0 trials from Meta caused by the installs goal, the app, or the offer? Question 2: how do apps that make a lot of money convert people (onboarding, paywall, prices, audience)?

## Read in this order
1. **RAHMAN-DECISIONS.md** - what Rahman decided to keep after going through every report (this is what to build).
2. **G-CHECKER-VERDICT.md** - checked claims, settled disagreements, the verdict (offer/audience ~40%, app ~35%, Meta installs goal ~25%), a comparison table and the top 10 changes.
3. The agent reports:
   - A-ai-calorie-apps.md - Cal AI and its clones: how they make money, onboarding, paywall, price, why people pay for clone #5
   - B-apps-like-ours.md - BetterMe Chair Yoga, Yoga-Go, WalkFit, Muscle Booster, pain apps, our competitor (Meta Ad Library)
   - C-onboarding-teardowns.md - 18 top apps' onboarding (Noom, Flo, Calm, Duolingo, Cal AI...)
   - D-paywall-numbers.md - Adapty / RevenueCat / Superwall benchmarks, US Health & Fitness, prices, Apple sheet completion
   - E-meta-to-trials.md - how apps growing on Meta get trials; standard vs custom events; web funnels; Apple US rules
   - F-walkito-roast.md - roast of Walkito 1.0.13, screen by screen, 15 ranked fixes with copy
   - H-web-quiz-funnels.md - 8 web quizzes walked through to the price page (competitor heelpaintrainer.com/quiz, BetterMe, WalkFit, Lasta, Simple, StretchPartner, backpain.ai, Noom)
   - I-screensdesign.md - 31 apps making $100K+/month from screensdesign.com
4. `evidence/` - screenshots the agents saved (prefix = agent letter). `walkito-1013-screens/` - Rahman's 1.0.13 screenshots.
5. 00-BRIEF.md - the brief the agents got.
