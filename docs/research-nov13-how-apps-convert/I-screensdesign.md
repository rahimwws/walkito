# I - ScreensDesign: how top-earning apps get from first screen to purchase

Agent I, Nov 13, 2026. Source: screensdesign.com, free tier only. No login, no Pro, no purchase.

**What I could see for free (all checked myself):**
- For every app: revenue and installs estimates, the full **tagged step list ("Chapters")** with timestamps, and a short text caption for every recorded screen. Captions are public in the page, including screens whose image is locked.
- **Images:** only the first ~8 screens (the first 26 seconds of the recording) plus 6 "featured" screens per app are unblurred. Everything after 26 s, including most paywalls, is blurred behind "Unlock Pro". I did not try to get around that.
- Paywall details below come from two kinds of evidence. **[verified]** means I saw the unblurred image. **[reported-SD]** means it comes from ScreensDesign's own text caption of a blurred screen.
- **Locked filters:** the Revenue, Paywall Type and Onboarding Steps filters are all Pro. So I picked apps by name from the public sitemap (2,930 apps) and read each app page.
- **"Hard/soft" and "trial"** are not labels in the free data. I classified them myself from captions, chapter names and known business models. Each one is marked.

Revenue figures are ScreensDesign estimates (monthly, worldwide) as shown on Nov 13, 2026. Treat them as **[reported]** and expect +/- 30% or more.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Walkito's flow is not unusual. It matches the median top earner.** In 31 apps making $100K+/month, the paywall sits at **tagged step 21** (median), after **28 recorded screens** and **~2.3 minutes** of a fast tester's recording. Walkito's **30 screens before Day 1** is the same size as the women/easy-fitness quiz apps that make $200K-$1M:
  - Yoga-Go: 38 screens
  - WalkFit: 32
  - BetterMe: 39
  - FitMe: 44
  - JustFit: 67

  Length and order are not why Walkito gets 0 trials. [verified count]
- **Walkito's 3-page trial paywall is a copy of a winning pattern, not a mistake.**
  - **Cal AI ($2M/mo):** "Try Now", then a reminder page, then "Start your 3-day FREE trial to continue" with a timeline and Monthly $9.99 / Yearly $29.99.
  - **Ladder ($6M):** a trial timeline, then "Yes, Remind Me".
  - **Finch ($1M):** two "See my FREE offer" pages, then a 7-day timeline.

  [verified Cal AI image; others reported-SD]
- **The thing top apps have that Walkito does not: a second, cheaper offer after the user says no.**
  - At least 9 of 31 do this: Bend, Yazio (spin-the-wheel, 75% off), Flo ("Special Offer Paywall"), Finch ("One-time offer applied", $69.99 crossed out to $34.99), JustFit ("Skip free trial and receive your discount"), Impulse (gift boxes, 92% off), Fastic (75% off), Simple ("welcome offer"), Calm (crossed-out anchor).
  - Walkito 1.0.13 removed its only second offer (the $29.99 countdown). [verified images for Finch, JustFit, Bend; reported-SD for others]
- **Price anchoring is everywhere. Walkito shows none.**
  - Calm: ~~$179.88~~ $69.99/yr = **$5.84/mo**, next to Monthly $14.99. That is 12 x $14.99, the same monthly price Walkito charges. [verified]
  - Bend: ~~$59.99~~ $29.99/yr = $2.49/mo. [verified]
  - GOWOD: ~~$143.88~~ $107.99/yr. [verified]
  - Walkito shows "$59.99 per year" with no per-month price and no crossed-out anchor. [verified IMG_6947]
- **Every top women/easy-fitness quiz app uses a hard paywall:** Yoga-Go, WalkFit, Muscle Booster, BetterMe, JustFit, FitMe (6 of 6). Across all 31 apps it is 17 hard to 14 soft.
  - Soft paywalls belong to big brands with a real free product: MyFitnessPal, Strava, Duolingo, Lose It, Lifesum, Finch, Hevy.
  - Walkito's hard paywall fits its niche. [classification = guess from captions and known models]
- **Free trial is the norm (22 of 31), but not in the cheapest-to-copy funnel.**
  - WellTech's Yoga-Go ($200K), WalkFit ($300K) and Muscle Booster ($400K) show **3 paid plans (1, 3 and 12 months) with no trial** and a plain "Continue". They also sell on the web (R3).
  - Trial length among the winners: 3 days (Cal AI, Impulse, FitMe), **7 days (most)**, 14 days (Headspace, Flo, GOWOD), 30 days (MyFitnessPal, Strava).
- **No pain, physio or foot app in the library makes real money:**
  - Prehab ("93% get out of pain", $199.99/yr): $5K/mo
  - Reveri: $20K
  - Chair Yoga for Seniors: $6.8K
  - SeniorFit: $20K
  - Tai Chi for Beginners Seniors: $21K

  The 45+ apps that earn $200K-$1M (Yoga-Go, WalkFit, JustFit, FitMe, BetterMe) sell **weight and body shape** with body-type quizzes, "30 million users" social proof and weight-projection charts. Walkito's health-ad rules ban most of that. **[verified revenue labels; the conclusion is a guess]**
- **What Walkito already has that no top app has:** a real phone measurement before the paywall (Foot Check), a free real session offered before the price, and a named PT reviewer.
  - Closest equivalents: JustFit has a "Flexibility Test" and "Cardio Function Test", but they are quiz questions, not measurements. GOWOD's mobility test is a long free in-app feature. Duolingo's first lesson comes after its paywall.
  - These are Walkito's real advantages. They are currently hidden on the paywall (see F-walkito-roast.md).

## 4. What Walkito should copy or test (ranked)

Each change must follow the health-ad rules: no cure or relief claims, no timeframes, no before/after, no fake numbers.

1. **Add an honest anchor and a per-month price on page 3.**
   - **Copy:** Yearly ~~$179.88~~ **$59.99 a year = $5 a month** next to Monthly $14.99.
   - **Proof:** Calm ($3M/mo) uses exactly this, built from its own $14.99 monthly price, and Walkito's monthly price is also $14.99. Bend, GOWOD, Finch, Chair Yoga and SeniorFit all show the per-month number.
   - **Expected effect:** better plan perception for a price-shy 55+ buyer. [guess, common in winners]
   - **Effort:** S (copy + layout).
2. **Bring back a second offer when she says no, without the countdown.**
   - **Copy:** after "Not now" on page 3, or when "What stopped you?" = "price": "Your first year for $39.99" (a real, permanent lower yearly product), or a 3-month plan.
   - **Proof:** at least 9 of 31 winners do this. Finch's "One-time offer applied / Skip this one-time offer" is the calmest version.
   - **Expected effect:** catches some price objectors. It will not fix trust objectors. [guess]
   - **Effort:** S-M (one new IAP product + one screen).
3. **Put the user's own numbers and the PT on the paywall.** (Walkito's real advantage. Agrees with F.)
   - **What:** the Foot Check result ("Left ankle bends 6 degrees less") and Dr. Carla's face on page 1 or 3.
   - **Proof:** Finch and Lose It put reviews on the PW. Bend puts reviews and community there. BetterMe puts an expert card on its upsell. Walkito has no reviews to show, but it has a measurement and a credential nobody else has.
   - **Effort:** S-M.
4. **Ask for an App Store rating right after the Day 1 session (only after a good session).**
   - **Proof:** Bend, Yazio and Cal AI ask for a rating before their paywall. Walkito has 4 ratings, which is weak proof on the App Store page every Meta user passes through.
   - **Expected effect:** more ratings over weeks, not trials this week. [guess]
   - **Effort:** S.
5. **Test a 3-month option as the middle plan** ($24.99 already exists in App Store Connect per F).
   - **Proof:** JustFit, FitMe ("Popular"), Simple, Yoga-Go, Muscle Booster and WalkFit all show 3 months. That is the 45+ women funnel exactly.
   - **Risk:** no trial on it, and it splits attention. [guess]
   - **Effort:** S.
6. **Move "How did you find Walkito?" after purchase and fix the notification line.**
   - Ask for notifications at trial start, as Headspace and Ladder do: "Want a reminder 2 days before your trial ends?"
   - **Effort:** S. Small effect. [guess]
7. **A safe progress chart instead of the pain "Outlook".**
   - Chart what the plan does, not what the pain does: "Week 1 seated, Week 2 standing, Week 3 longer holds", or the user's ankle-bend re-test.
   - **Proof:** 10 of 31 winners use a projection chart. Yoga-Go and WalkFit place it right before the building screen.
   - **Effort:** M. [guess on effect]
8. **Do NOT copy:**
   - before/after photos, "Limited time offer 01:00:00" countdowns and "93% get out of pain" (the senior and pain apps that do this earn $5K-$20K/mo)
   - body-type silhouettes, weight targets, "30 million users"

   They break Walkito's ad and health rules, and they did not make those niche apps rich. [verified revenue; conclusion guess]

**What this says about the two brief questions:**
- **Q1 (installs goal or the app?):** ScreensDesign cannot see traffic quality. What it shows is that Walkito's onboarding length, paywall position, 7-day trial, hard paywall and 3-page trial paywall all fall inside what $1M+ apps do. So the flow shape is an unlikely sole cause of 0 trials. The bigger gaps are trust and price framing on the paywall (no anchor, no proof, no second offer), and possibly the traffic itself. [guess, consistent with the evidence]
- **Q2 (how big apps convert):** the 45+ money is made by broad weight/body apps with long quizzes. Pain-specific apps in this library all earn under $25K/mo. Walkito competes in a niche where nobody in the library has proven big subscription revenue yet. [verified revenue labels; interpretation guess]
