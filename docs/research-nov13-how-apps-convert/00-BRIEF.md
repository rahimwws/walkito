## Walkito facts (true as of Nov 13, 2026)

- iPhone app "Walkito: Heel Pain & Flat Feet" (App Store id 6813076846), US. Short daily foot exercises (3-10 min) for heel pain, plantar fasciitis, flat feet, people on their feet all day. Seller Nemy LLC.
- Prices: yearly $59.99 with 7-day free trial (preselected), monthly $14.99 (no trial), lifetime behind "Show all plans". Hard paywall.
- **Meta ads Nov 9-12:** ~$190 spent, ~72 installs (~$2.35 each), Advantage+ app campaign, goal = App installs (Start trial goal is NOT eligible on iPhone, too few trial events). Audience: 83% age 55+, 75% women, ~93% Facebook (Reels + Feed). One ad got ~95% of spend: a cartoon-mascot video comparing "free videos" with Walkito. Campaign now paused.
- **Funnel, real US Meta users on 1.0.11-1.0.12 (~25 people):** almost all finished onboarding (3-10 min). ~15 closed the app on paywall page 1 ("Try Walkito free for 7 days", only a "Try it free" button, no "Not now" on those versions). 2 left on page 2 (reminder page, first time $59.99 is shown). 4 left on page 3 (prices). 3 tapped "Not now" and did the free Day 1 session. 4 tapped a buy button overall; all closed Apple's payment sheet. **0 trials from Meta.** Apple's sheet was checked Nov 13 and correctly shows "1-week trial, then $59.99/year".
- Only 1 real trial so far, from a Czech organic user. Only ~27% allow tracking (ATT).
- **1.0.13 (live Nov 12) already changed:** "Not now" + plain price line on page 1, monthly shown, $29.99 countdown offer removed, "What stopped you?" after a closed Apple sheet, seated Day 1, a "Foot Check" (ankle bend + balance test by phone, 2 of 5 results locked behind the paywall). No Meta user has used 1.0.13 yet.
- Flow in 1.0.13 (screenshots in `walkito-1013-screens/`): "Your plan is ready" (mascot, "Exercises reviewed by Dr. Carla Shayman, PT") -> "Try the first 2 minutes now" (Start Day 1 / Not now) -> paywall page 1 "Try Walkito free for 7 days" -> page 2 "We'll remind you 2 days before your trial ends" (says "Walkito Plus") -> page 3 "7 days free. Then you decide." (light purple, clouds, mascot, yearly/monthly). "Not now" -> Day 1 session -> tutorial -> prices again (hard paywall over the app).
