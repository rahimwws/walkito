# What Rahman decided to keep (Nov 13-14, 2026)

Rahman went through all 8 research reports one by one and kept the items below. This is the build list. Report letters in brackets show where each idea came from.

## A. Paywall (in the app)
1. **Remove paywall page 2** ("We'll remind you 2 days before your trial ends"). Its info is already on page 1 and in the page-3 timeline. [I, C]
2. **Show HER result right before the price** - her own Foot Check numbers (for example "Your left ankle bends 6 degrees less than your right. Balance: 12 seconds."), not the generic "Your plan is ready" bars. No dates, no promised outcomes. [A, C]
3. **Paywall look:** a real foot clip + Dr. Carla Shayman's photo, dark style like the onboarding, instead of the mascot (gift box, alarm clock) and the purple clouds. **Remove "Most popular"** (not true yet). Get Dr. Carla's written OK before her photo is used bigger. [F]
4. **One line right above the buy button:** "Next, Apple asks you to confirm. You pay $0.00 today." (0 of 4 Meta buy taps confirmed Apple's sheet; normal is 50-70%.) [D, F]

## B. Prices
5. **Lower prices: $49.99 a year (7-day free trial) and $9.99 a month** (like the competitor). US Health & Fitness median is $35.99/yr and $9.99/mo; our $59.99/$14.99 is above the top quarter. [D, A]
6. **Show yearly per month:** "about $4 a month" big, with "billed $49.99 per year" under it (Apple: the billed amount must stay clear). [A, I]
7. **"$4.99 first month" on the monthly plan, shown on the prices page from the start** (Apple introductory offer: first month $4.99, then $9.99). [B, H, I]
8. **Lifetime visible** as a smaller card under yearly, label "Pay once, no subscription" (about $79.99-99.99, it exists today at $99.99). Not hidden behind "Show all plans". [D]
9. **After someone closes Apple's payment sheet:** only "What stopped you?" (already in 1.0.13). If the answer is price, remind them of the $4.99 first month / lifetime that were already on the page. **Nothing new or cheaper appears, nothing after "Not now", no timer** (Apple Guideline 5.6). No fake crossed-out "was" price. [I, G]

Planned prices page: Yearly $49.99 (7 days free, about $4 a month, preselected) / Monthly first month $4.99, then $9.99 / Lifetime "Pay once, no subscription".

## C. App
10. **Day 1: add "Skip" / "Later", and let her scroll the app** (her 7-day plan, exercises, Foot Check) with the sessions locked, instead of being stuck in Day 1. Day 1 stays free; tapping any other session opens the paywall. Still a hard paywall: look, don't use. [Rahman's own test]
11. **Fix the odd labels:** "Right shin" on a heel-pain app, the unexplained "First steps 5/10", "Choosing your first week..." (looks like it is still loading), and "Your plan is ready" 3 screens in a row. [C]

## D. Meta (for when ads restart - Rahman decides the restart)
12. **Standard Meta events** (Meta does not support custom events for iPhone ads): "saw the price" = **View content**, "tapped buy" = **Initiate checkout**, through AppsFlyer. No health words in event names or parameters. [E]
13. **Ads built on one specific, real moment** ("the first steps out of bed", "after 10 hours on your feet"), not a general benefit (about 4x cheaper per trial in another app). Real people with signed releases or real feet, no AI people. [E, B]
14. **Facebook only, no Audience Network.** [E]

## E. Web quiz funnel - good idea (Rahman)
Why: every winner with women 55+ from Facebook does it (BetterMe, Yoga-Go, WalkFit, Muscle Booster, and since Nov 11 our competitor); on the web Meta sees who finished the quiz and who paid (no Apple tracking limits), so it can learn to find buyers instead of installers; a small paid first month is easy and prices change without Apple review; the quiz sells before the install. [B, E, H]
15. **Style:** calm "pain app" style like the competitor / StretchPartner / backpain.ai - 15-20 screens, age first, honest, no timers, no scratch cards, no body pictures.
16. **"Try one move, rate before and after"** before the price (StretchPartner: rate stiffness 1-5, a 55-second seated ankle move, rate again, then plan and price). Can also be used in the app with Day 1.
17. **An email step** ("Save your plan") to follow up with people who did not pay.
18. **A small first payment: $4.99 first month.**
Risks: Meta may treat a heel-pain site as health and block the Purchase event; the pay-then-download step for 55+; refunds, chargebacks, online cancel and US auto-renewal rules; Apple's 0% link-out could change (Supreme Court hearing Jan 11, 2027). **Rahim owns all payments, refunds and support. Rahman cannot operate payments (F-1).** Plan: run it in parallel with the fixed app, split the Meta budget, compare cost per paying user.

## F. For Rahim
19. **Download many successful apps** (Cal AI, BetterMe, Yoga-Go, WalkFit, Calm, Finch, Flo, Noom, the competitor Plantar Fasciitis Trainer...) and run their onboarding and paywalls through your own agency system. Bring back what to copy.

## Not now / skipped
- Android-first ads (discuss later; Google Play held until January).
- A 3-month middle plan (later).
- A video of the app working as the first screen.
- Paid creators for 55+ (later).
- Never copy: before/after, countdowns, scratch cards, AI people, weight/date promises, fake numbers or crossed-out prices.
