# Research brief: psychology of onboarding and paywalls (Walkito, Nov 9, 2026)

## The app
Walkito: Heel Pain & Flat Feet (iOS, App Store id 6813076846). Subscription foot-exercise app for heel pain / plantar fasciitis / flat feet. US, English. Users mostly 35-65+.
Flow today: long personal quiz onboarding (questions about feet, pain, job, goals, "where did you hear about us") > custom plan > App Tracking prompt > 3-screen hard paywall (screen 1, screen 2, plans) > yearly $59.99 with 7-day free trial for Meta users (full price for others) > "Not now" leads to a one-time offer screen. No access to the app without starting a trial or paying.

## The problem (real data, Nov 9)
Meta ads bring installs at about $3 each. Of 42 people who started onboarding, 22 finished, 21 reached the plans screen, 19 of them saw "1 week free". 0 tapped "Start free week", 0 tapped "Not now". Most left within 3-25 seconds of seeing the plans. 10 people rage-tapped somewhere. Organic App Store users in October did start trials (about 5% of installs).

## What we want
Evidence-based ways to use human psychology in onboarding and on the paywall so more people start the free week and later pay, especially COLD paid-social users (Meta), in a HEALTH context, ethically and within Apple and US rules.
