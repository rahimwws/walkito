## Real funnel data, Nov 9 (Meta traffic, PostHog)
- 42 started onboarding > 22 finished > 22 saw the paywall > 21 reached the plans screen (step 3).
- 19 of 22 saw the free-week offer (they answered Facebook/Instagram). 3 saw full price.
- 0 tapped "Start my free trial". 0 tapped "Not now" (paywall_skipped = 0). 0 closed the paywall (paywall_dismissed = 0). Most left the app 3-25 seconds after the plans screen.
- 1 person on the full-price paywall tapped buy, then closed Apple's sheet.
- 10 people had rage-clicks ($rageclick, screen name logged only as "RNSScreen").
- On earlier days a few people per day did tap "Not now".
- 16 of 21 tapped "Don't allow" on tracking.
- 21 of 22 paywall viewers were on app version 1.0.10.
