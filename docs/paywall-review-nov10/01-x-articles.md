# Onboarding and paywall: what 7 X posts/articles say (collected Nov 9, 2026)

Source type: founders' own posts on X. Self-reported numbers, not verified. Use as ideas to check, not proof.

## 1. @Siron93 - "100 Lessons From Bootstrapping My App to a 7-Figure Exit"
https://x.com/Siron93/status/1990410583024308449 (1.3K saves)

Onboarding:
- Onboarding is the most important flow. One shot to convince.
- Sell benefits, not features.
- Guide the user straight to the "aha" moment.
- Never put sign-up before the user sees value or the paywall (sign-up = one of the biggest drop-offs).
- Personalized onboarding (a few questions) beat generic in all their tests.
- Progress bar is a must for multi-step onboarding.
- Trust signals: real testimonials, ratings, user counts. Do not fake them.
- Loading screens: show testimonials, ratings, quick wins instead of a spinner.
- Name the pain first, then show the app as the cure.
- Small commitments: "Are you ready to improve your health?" (yes), a small ritual (sign, tap "I promise").
- "Do you relate?" statements frame the pain.
- After each answer, a short supportive screen ("Great choice", "We've got you covered").
- Every extra field (name, email, phone) is a drop-off point.

Paywall:
- Show the paywall during onboarding. Highest share of future payers is there, at peak motivation.
- Show the paywall again on app open; people may not be ready the first time.
- Hard vs soft paywall: must be tested. For them hard paywall (no access without trial) won.
- Test radical prices ($39.99 vs $99.99/yr).
- Weekly plan lowered risk and more people jumped in (but slower to recover ad spend; watch 1-2 months of cohorts).
- Lifetime offer priced a bit above average LTV worked.
- Soft paywall: show a discount on exit attempt.
- After a trial starts, show what they unlocked and guide to the best feature.
- Win-back: users who cancelled a trial get a "welcome back" offer next time they open the app.

Measurement:
- Funnel report for every onboarding step; fix the screen with the biggest drop first.
- Remote config for prices, texts, flags, so changes do not need app review.
- "How did you hear about us" screen for multi-channel ads.

## 2. @kenbuildsalone - his paywall numbers
https://x.com/kenbuildsalone/status/2104466852525289594
- Interactive onboarding: 17.9% start a trial after completing an in-app demo vs 7.8% if they skip it.
- Hard paywall + 7-day free trial.
- 3-screen paywall: adding 2 screens before pricing raised trial conversion from 8.1% to 27.5%.
  - Screen 1: help users imagine a few weeks with the app.
  - Screen 2: list every feature.
  - Screen 3: pricing.
- Problem left: 76% of trials cancel.

## 3. @askwhykartik - "$100k/$300k-a-month onboarding structure"
https://x.com/askwhykartik/status/2092590131605569556
Flow: Quiz > Results > Symptoms > How app can help > Reviews > App features > Custom plan > Paywall > (if declined) Discounted paywall.
- Quiz: user invests before seeing a feature.
- Results: show where they stand (emotional hook, e.g. "your X is higher than average").
- Symptoms / pain screens: name the pain, make them feel understood.
- How app helps: the fix, after they felt the problem.
- Reviews: social proof when self-doubt peaks.
- Features: confirm, not sell.
- Custom plan: "built for you" (claims 3x better than generic pricing).
- Paywall + discounted paywall: full price for the ready, discount for fence-sitters.
- Example paywall in the image: "Start your 3-day FREE trial to continue" with a timeline (Today / in 2 days reminder / day 3 billing), rating stars, reviews.

## 4. @StevenCravotta - "paywalls that made $40k/mo"
https://x.com/StevenCravotta/status/1878531409834397937 (1.1K saves)
1. Hard paywall: clear paywall upfront with a trial option beats "maybe later".
2. Social proof: testimonials, stats, streaks.
3. Split test headlines, designs, offers (e.g. Superwall).
4. Study competitors' paywalls (e.g. Mobbin).
5. Clear value: users must instantly know their transformation.
6. Onboarding primes the purchase: walk users through their own problem so they are ready to commit.

## 5. @guillemcraft - trials that did not convert
https://x.com/guillemcraft/status/2117530541478064493
https://x.com/guillemcraft/status/2117575767479754987
- 94 trials ended, 5 paid. 56 of 85 cancelled the same day they started. "The decision happens on the paywall."
- 22 of his last 29 paying users never took the trial; they paid directly.
- Tip: App Store Connect subscription event report has "days before canceling". Mostly 0 = the trial is not doing its job.
- Next update: no free trial, weekly plan first and preselected, close button back (people can look around, main feature needs a subscription), closing shows a one-time offer (that offer brought 1 in 6 paying users). Hard paywall test: day one no lift.

## 6. @itsbongoo - checklist before quitting an app
https://x.com/itsbongoo/status/2119871030747701522
Product side:
- Rewrote onboarding at least 5 times and measured drop-off?
- Tested paywall: prices, plans, trial vs no trial, headline, layout?
- Tried weekly, yearly, and yearly-with-trial?
- Redone App Store screenshots 3+ times, first one sells the result?
- Shown a different offer to people who closed the paywall, next time they open the app?
- Watched someone use the app in person without helping?
Marketing side: 100+ short videos, 20+ hooks, 3+ faceless accounts, 10+ UGC creators, Apple Search Ads on competitors and own keywords, enough budget and weeks for paid ads to learn, DM 50 micro-influencers.

## 7. Paul Solt - "$250 to $5K MRR: 5 App Store Packaging Rules" (X article, 427K views)
https://x.com/PaulSolt/status/2045580498232373433
- Onboarding: 3-4 screens, show the outcome, not a tutorial. One action per step. Reinforce the App Store screenshot message. Videos convert better than static images. Goal: reach "aha" fast.
- Paywall: ask for money in the first session (75% of Viktor Seraleev's revenue). Put it right after the value moment. No hidden close button, no fake urgency, no price tricks. Clear offer, clear value.
  - Test 3-day, 7-day, 30-day trials. Weekly $5.99-7.99, annual $29.99-59.99.
  - Use comparison and easy numbers: annual vs weekly savings, price per week, App Store rating for trust.
  - "Don't make a user think twice about your pricing."
- Flow: one screen, one action, remove anything that makes them stop and think.

## Combined checklist to compare Walkito against
Onboarding
1. Pain named first, app shown as the fix?
2. Personal quiz with a "results" screen (where they stand)?
3. Real social proof (ratings, reviews, user count) before the paywall?
4. Custom plan "built for you" right before the paywall?
5. Short: one action per screen, progress bar, no extra fields, no sign-up before paywall?
6. Shows the outcome (video of exercises / result), not a tutorial?
7. Per-step drop-off measured?

Paywall
8. 1-2 value screens before the price (imagine weeks with the app, what you get)?
9. Outcome/transformation obvious in one glance?
10. Free trial clear (timeline: today free, reminder before billing, billing day)?
11. Easy price math (per week, savings vs monthly/weekly)?
12. Trust: rating, reviews, "cancel anytime"?
13. Visible, honest close / "Not now", and a one-time offer after closing?
14. Offer again on next app open for people who closed?
15. Plans tested: yearly with trial vs weekly vs no trial?
16. Nothing broken or slow (buttons respond on first tap)?
