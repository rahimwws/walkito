# The Meta ads that bring the traffic (live since Nov 9, 2026)
Campaign "WK | iOS | US | Installs | Nov9": App installs goal, $50/day, US, age 25+ (suggested 35-65), broad, iOS. All 4 ads send people to a custom App Store product page, then the app.
Spend so far is almost all on V2_mascot (Nov 9: V2 $55.79 of $59.51, 19 installs at ~$2.94).

## Creatives (files in this folder)
- **V1_morning** (video, `final_V1_morning.jpg`, `V1_cover_4x5.jpg`): morning, edge of the bed, "Before your first step" foot exercises.
- **V2_mascot** (video, `final_V2_mascot.jpg`): side-by-side "Free videos" vs "Walkito" with the mascot. Gets ~95% of spend.
- **I1_receipt** (image, `Image1-receipt-1x1-feed.png`): receipt "$1,240 on heel pain treatments." / "$59.99 fixed it."
- **I2_kayla_chat** (image, `Image2-kayla-chat-4x5-feed.png`): iMessage-style chat from "Kayla" about her feet in the morning.

## Primary text (3 versions on every ad, Meta rotates)
A - Morning:
"If the first few steps after you wake up are the worst part of your day, this is for you. Your foot muscles get stiff overnight, and almost nobody trains them. Walkito gives you 5 minutes of easy exercises to do before you get out of bed, and changes them depending on how your feet feel that morning. First week is free, then $59.99 a year."

B - Tried everything:
"Insoles didn't fix it. Neither did the night splint. Here's why. Insoles support your foot, but they don't make it stronger. The small muscles under your arch do most of the work when you stand and walk, and almost nobody trains them. Walkito is 5 minutes a day of exercises for exactly that. Try the first week free, then it's $59.99 a year."

C - Long shift:
"Nurses, this one's for you. (And anyone else on their feet for 12 hours.) Standing all day wears out the small muscles in your feet long before the rest of you gets tired. Walkito gives you 5 minutes of foot exercises to do before your shift, and tells you exactly which ones based on how your feet feel that day. First week free, then $59.99 a year."

Headlines: "5 minutes a day for your feet" / "Do this before your first step"

## Message-match notes (data, Nov 9)
- Ads lead with morning heel pain, but only 1 of 49 users picked "Easier mornings" as their goal; 16 of 36 tapped "Nothing hurts right now" on the pain map.
- The ad text promises "First week free, then $59.99 a year"; on the live paywall the free week is in a small line inside the plan card and under the button.
