# Wave 1B brief: what other founders did (community research)

Read `00-BRIEF.md` in this folder first (the app and the rules). Update from Wave 1A: the only trial since Nov 9 was a tester, so real trials = 0 from ~64 Meta users. On the newest version 97% finish onboarding but 13 of 23 leave at the first paywall screen; the 3 who tapped "Start free week" all closed Apple's payment sheet.

## Every community agent answers the same 4 questions, with REAL cases
1. "Lots of installs from Meta/TikTok, almost no trials": who had it, what caused it, what fixed it?
2. People finish onboarding but leave at the paywall, or close Apple's payment sheet: what changed it?
3. Pricing: weekly vs monthly vs yearly as the main plan, trial vs no trial, price tests ($29.99 / $39.99 / $59.99 / weekly $4.99-9.99), intro offers, with numbers.
4. What actually worked, with before -> after numbers.

## Output format for every case
App type | who (handle/link) | problem | what they changed | before -> after numbers | sample size if given | date | URL | credibility (STRONG/MEDIUM/WEAK).
Prefer 2025-2026 cases, health/fitness/wellness first, then other consumer subscription apps. Only keep cases with something concrete. Also log failures (what did NOT work).
