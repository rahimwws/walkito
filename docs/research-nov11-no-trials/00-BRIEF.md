# Research brief: Meta brings installs, almost no trials (Walkito, Nov 11, 2026)

## The app
Walkito: Heel Pain & Flat Feet. iPhone (App Store id 6813076846) subscription foot-exercise app for heel pain, plantar fasciitis, flat feet. US, English. Hard paywall. Yearly $59.99 with 7-day free trial. Other plans behind "Show all plans" (monthly, lifetime). One-time offer after "Not now". Users mostly women 45-65+ who walk or stand all day.

## The problem (real data)
- Meta installs campaign since Nov 9: Advantage+ app campaign, iOS, US, age 25+ (suggested 35-65), broad, $50/day, goal = app installs (Start trial is not eligible on iPhone yet). ~$164 spent, 66 installs at ~$2.48. 95% of spend on one ad (V2_mascot: side-by-side "Free videos" vs "Walkito" with a mascot).
- Meta users (answered Facebook/Instagram in the app survey), Nov 9-11: 65 started onboarding, 50 finished, 50 saw the paywall, 35 reached the plans screen with the price, 3 tapped "Start free week", **0 trials**. One trial on Nov 11 from a user who answered "TikTok" (may be Meta).
- App versions: 1.0.10 (Nov 9-10) had a paywall with no visible exit. 1.0.11 (Nov 10 evening) and 1.0.12 (Nov 11) added: no swipe gate, no signature, trial screens before plans ("Try Walkito free for 7 days", reminder screen), visible "Not now", free week for everyone. On the new versions about half the users leave at paywall step 1, which has no "Not now".
- Benchmark we keep hearing: health apps get roughly 5-15% install to trial. We are at 0-1 of ~65.
